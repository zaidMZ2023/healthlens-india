# HealthLens India product research log

Verification date: 2026-09-19

Method: Official Indian manufacturer pages first; otherwise FSSAI-style nutrition text from trusted Indian retailers (Tata 1mg, SastaSundar, ZydusMart, Swiggy Instamart, Zepto). Blogs, calorie apps, Open Food Facts, and non-India labels were not used as recorded values. Powder values are powder-only.

## Cadbury Bournvita Original

- **Exact variant researched:** Cadbury Bournvita Original cereal-based nutrition drink powder, 500 g jar (listing also notes +50 g extra free; net listed as 550 g on one page; recorded pack is 500 g as the named SKU).
- **Source URL:** https://sastasundar.com/order-otc/bournvita-powder-jar-50-g-extra-free-500-g-r43u7dy
- **Source type:** Retailer (SastaSundar). Cadbury India site was a “launching soon” placeholder and did not publish a panel.
- **Nutrition found (per 100 g powder):** Energy 381 kcal; protein 7 g; total sugars 46 g; added sugars 32.2 g; dietary fibre 3.5 g; sodium 175 mg. Serving: 2 heaped spoonfuls (20 g) in 200 ml milk.
- **Uncertainty:** Other SastaSundar SKUs list 387 kcal and 49.8 g total sugars, and Tata 1mg lists 393 kcal without total sugar. Formulation appears pack/batch dependent. Status set to **Verified** for this specific 500 g jar listing because that page has a complete core panel.
